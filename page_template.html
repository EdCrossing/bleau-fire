Which Bleau boulders burned

On 12 July 2026 an arson fire burned roughly 2,000 hectares of the Fontainebleau massif — about a tenth of the forest, and the worst since records began in 1863. Every one of __N_TOTAL__ bouldering problems in Boolder's open database is sampled against the burn, so the answer is a named problem on a named circuit rather than a hectare count.

Fuel typeha in fireburneddNBRRdNBR

The opposite of what I predicted. The textbook argument is that dNBR under-reads sparse fuel and RdNBR compresses the gap. Heath reads highest here, closed conifer lowest, and RdNBR widens the spread. Calluna heath at Fontainebleau sits on open sand and burns to complete consumption, exposing bright substrate that is highly reflective in SWIR — so over heath the index substantially measures the sand revealed, not the energy released.

A null result worth keeping

What did not explain severity

Given the fire reached a place, what determined how hard it burned there? Elevation, slope, aspect, distance to the track network, pre-fire vegetation condition and fuel class, over 60,000 pixels sampled from inside the footprint eroded by 120 m.

ValidationR²What it means
Random 5-fold+0.392Looks like a working model
Spatially blocked 5-fold−0.185Worse than predicting the mean

A negative R² means no transferable skill at all. The random score was measuring spatial autocorrelation — with shuffled folds, test pixels sit metres from training pixels, so the model scores well by recognising the neighbourhood. The tell was the random folds being implausibly tight: 0.394, 0.397, 0.386, 0.387, 0.395. Together with the weather result this says the informative variance for this fire is in the when, not the where within.

What the data will not support

The fire footprint is mine, not an official perimeter

The red outline is derived here — gaps in the dNBR ≥ 0.27 mask closed, interior holes filled, components under 200 ha dropped. It exists so the fuel comparison only counts polygons the fire actually reached. Copernicus EMS published a real delineation under activation EMSR894; until that is wired in, treat the outline as a working boundary.

Half the naive burned area is farm harvest

Fields cut between the two dates produce the same signature dNBR detects. Against IGN's RPG register of declared parcels: of the 3,711 ha above dNBR 0.10, 1,867 ha sits on farmland. At 0.27 that falls to 118 ha. The forest-only figure is __FOREST_HA__ ha.

Contributed positions, not survey

Boolder's coordinates come from climbers, not surveyors — 10–20 m of error, which is one to two pixels. Each problem is sampled over a 50 m window and flagged where the plausible range crosses a class boundary. More problems does not mean more precise ones: a circuit-level percentage is far more trustworthy than any single problem's class.
